Privacy Policy

BrickSculpt · Last updated November 2026

BrickSculpt works entirely on your iPhone and iPad. There is no BrickSculpt account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. BrickSculpt does not collect any data, and we never receive anything from your device.

What the app does on your device

BrickSculpt works out the brick layout, the 3-D preview, the building steps and the parts list on your device. It does not use the internet to do this, and it never asks for access to your contacts, location or microphone.

Camera and photos (brick scanning)

If you choose to scan your bricks, BrickSculpt asks for the camera, or you pick a single photo with Apple’s photo picker, which gives the app only that photo. The photo is analyzed on your device to count the bricks in it and is then discarded: it is not saved by the app, and it is never uploaded or sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in Settings > BrickSculpt.

Your brick list

The bricks you scan or add by hand are saved on your device so the app can show what you have and what you still need. That list stays on your device; we never receive it.

Purchases

Sculpture packs are bought with an in-app purchase, which Apple handles entirely. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; BrickSculpt never sees your name, Apple Account, card or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether a pack has been bought on your Apple Account, so it can unlock it, and Restore Purchases asks Apple the same thing. We receive no purchase data that identifies you.

Exporting and printing

When you tap Export or Printable Build Guide, BrickSculpt creates a 3-D model file, a parts wanted list, a parts spreadsheet or a PDF build guide on your device, and hands them to Apple’s share sheet. They go only where you choose to send or save them, such as Files, AirDrop or Mail. Any website you then upload them to has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

BrickSculpt keeps your brick list and temporary export files on your device. Use Remove All Bricks in My Bricks to clear the list, or delete the app to remove everything. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

BrickSculpt collects no data from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple, and Ask to Buy and Screen Time controls work as usual.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs your data, the updated version will be posted on this page before that version is released.
