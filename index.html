1. Look it over. Spin and zoom the model, studs and all. The first sculpture is a lying English Springer Spaniel in liver and white.
2. Pick a size. Slide from a desk-size 22 cm to a showpiece over 40 cm. The brick count, size and parts update instantly.
3. Build it. Step through each layer with a labelled plan that shows exactly which brick goes where, on top of the layer below.
4. Use what you have. Spread your bricks out and take a photo. BrickSculpt counts them by size and color, shows what you still need, and makes a shopping list of just the missing bricks.

Private by design. Everything, including brick scanning, is worked out on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads.
